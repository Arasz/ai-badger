# tutorials — learning-oriented, we choose the goal

A tutorial takes a newcomer through a chosen exercise to a guaranteed result. The author picks the
goal, not the reader, and every step is one the reader can complete without deciding anything. It
teaches confidence, not coverage — it may deliberately omit options and edge cases.

The moment the reader has their own goal, they want `../how-to/` instead.
