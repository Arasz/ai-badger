# 0.135.0 — Research-based prompt and persona optimization

Evidence-graded refactor of personas, prompts, and context files based on synthesis of 4 prompt-engineering research documents.

## What changed

**Personas (all 13 files):** Descriptions trimmed from 29-110 words to 7-19 words (task-naming line only). Research (Zheng et al., EMNLP 2024): irrelevant persona details cause up to 30pp degradation.

**code-reviewer.md:** Added third-person framing (SYCON Bench: -63.8% sycophancy) and escalation rule (Laban et al.: consolidated restart after 2 failed rounds).

**delegation.md.tmpl:** Added reasoning-model dispatch guidance — strip CoT scaffolding for opus/o-series; state goals not process.

**CLAUDE.md.tmpl + HERMES.md.tmpl:** Reordered for position effects (Lost in the Middle: head/tail strongest). Invariants moved to end (recency position), context sections moved up.

**documentation.instructions.md:** Added humanization rules (burstiness, banned AI vocabulary, active voice).

**Invariants:** `no-hand-rolled-crypto.md` and `no-hardcoded-secrets.md` rewritten as positive specifications.

**docs/research/:** Added prompt-engineering research documents (3 files) as framework-level evidence records.

## Evidence

- Laban et al. "LLMs Get Lost in Multi-Turn" (arXiv 2505.06120) — 39% degradation at 2 turns
- IFEval (Zhou et al., arXiv 2311.07911) — constraint-count compliance tax
- SYCON Bench (EMNLP Findings 2025) — third-person framing reduces sycophancy
- Zheng et al. (EMNLP Findings 2024) — personas don't improve accuracy
