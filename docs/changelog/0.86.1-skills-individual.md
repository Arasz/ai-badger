# 0.86.1 — Skills individual improvements (I1–I12)

The second PR of the skills-optimization sequence (Part B of the
[opt-skills plan](../work/2026-08-07-opt-skills-plan.md)): the twelve per-skill
improvements the conventions PR-1 deliberately deferred. Content only — no tooling, no hooks,
no scripts.

## What changed

- **I1 — `code-review-checklist`** split its two densest phases into references: the Security
  checklist (8 items + OpenAI-provenance note) now lives in `references/security.md` and the
  Observability checklist (5 items + Kotlin-provenance note) in `references/observability.md`,
  each replaced in the body by a one-line pointer with a disclosure condition. All 9
  `<!-- EXT:* -->` markers and the `<!-- MERGE_EXTENSIONS -->` sentinel keep their positions.
- **I2 — `mcp-index`** moved `## Server status` to `references/status.md` and
  `## Auto-tagging Heuristics` to `references/heuristics.md` (pointer quotes in the body), and
  gained a `## When NOT to Use` section.
- **I3 — `call-behaviorist`** moved the record/event/key tables (with the compact-JSON example
  and the `PIPE_BUF` constraint) to `references/record-format.md` and `### What the findings
  mean` to `references/findings.md`, and gained a final `## Verification Checklist`.
- **I4 — `den-refresh`** moved its error/fix table to `references/error-recovery.md` and gained
  a final `## Verification Checklist`.
- **I5 — `task`** gained `## When NOT to Use` and a four-bullet `## Gotchas` (worktree-less
  start, `keptBecause`, byte-stable context prefix, two-level dispatch).
- **I6 — `commit-reminder`** gained a four-bullet `## Gotchas` (new-high ratchet, count-clearing
  actions, `additionalContext`-only hook, `ensure_committed.py` exit-0 contract).
- **I7 — `feed-badger`** gained `## Gotchas` and a final `## Verification Checklist`.
- **I8 — `prompt-markers`** gained a four-bullet `## Gotchas`.
- **I9 — `welcome-ai-badger`** gained a final `## Verification Checklist`.
- **I10 — `maintain-agent-instructions`** gained `## When NOT to Use` and a final
  `## Verification Checklist`.
- **I11 — `ai-raccoon-memory`** gained a `## When NOT to Use` section (numbered sections
  unchanged).
- **I12 — `evidence-first-research`** loading conditions on its `## Files` section were
  implemented in the 0.85.0 PR (the plan's G2 wording deferred to I12); this release verifies
  and ships them as planned.
- The repo's own `config.json` `frameworkVersion` moved to 0.85.1, fixing the drift notice
  (scaffolded 0.84.0 vs framework 0.85.0); root `skills/` plugin copy and `.ai-badger/`
  regenerated.

## Behaviour changes worth knowing

- None for consumers: content only. Already-scaffolded projects pick the new skill bodies and
  reference files up on the next `den-refresh` (drift is self-healing by design — new
  `references/` files appear as `drift.changed`/`newItems`).
- G1's corpus gate (`## Gotchas` on all 23 skills) is now fully satisfied: the four skills
  deferred by PR-1 (task, commit-reminder, prompt-markers, feed-badger) carry their Gotchas
  sections.
