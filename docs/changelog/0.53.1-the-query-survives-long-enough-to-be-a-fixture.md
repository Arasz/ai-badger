# 0.53.1 — the query survives long enough to be a fixture

Closes [#219](https://github.com/Arasz/ai-badger/issues/219).

`debug_log.py` capped every field at 200 characters. Measured against the real
audit log, that dropped most of the retrieval telemetry before it could become
anything:

```
records=1269  retrieval=74
not harvestable: redacted=0  clipped=57  machine=0
harvestable: 12 distinct queries from 17 records
```

**57 of 74 retrieval records — 77% — were stored as a prefix rather than a
question.** Redaction dropped none of them; the cap dropped all of them. And the
loss was not uniform: the clipped slice *is* the long-query regime, which is
where the coverage-gate defect in #165 lived. A fixture set harvested under that
cap would have been blind to the case most worth measuring.

## What changed

The query now gets a larger share than the other fields, because it is the one a
fixture can be built from:

- `MAX_FIELD_CHARS` stays 200 for every other field.
- `MAX_QUERY_CHARS` is 2000.

## The bound is now enforced, not assumed

A record has to fit `PIPE_BUF` (4096 bytes) or a concurrent `O_APPEND` write can
interleave with it and corrupt both lines. Field caps alone never guaranteed
that — fourteen fields at 200 characters plus keys was simply assumed to fit, and
raising the query's share would have made the assumption false.

`_fit` now closes that: it measures the serialised record and halves whichever
field is longest until the line fits. Taking the space from the largest field
means the query is not penalised for a bloated `top` or `terms` list. The
invariant holds for any input, including a caller passing 50 KB in every field.

## The harvester had to move in step

`tooling/fixture_harvest.py` mirrors the cap rather than importing it, and it
treats a query stored *at* a cap as a prefix. Raising it naively would have made
every pre-0.53.1 record — all clipped at exactly 200 — look like a whole
question. `CLIP_LENGTHS` holds both 200 and 2000, so records written under either
cap stay unusable, and only those.

## Mirrors

`debug_log.py` is copied into 17 tracked locations across four deployment shapes.
All 17 carry the new constants, propagated by re-scaffolding rather than by hand;
`gates/scaffold_freshness_guard.py` (new in 0.53.0) would fail the build
otherwise.
